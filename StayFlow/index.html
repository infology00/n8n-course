Availability & Booking Logic

Reliable booking information, without guesswork.

Showing room details is straightforward. Confirming live availability requires access to a reliable booking source. The system will only confirm availability when that information can actually be verified.

▦

Connected Booking Engine

If your booking platform supports availability integration, guests can be directed to the relevant unit and date-selection process using the supported connection.

↗

Existing Booking Links

If only booking links are available, the automation can ask for preferred dates and send guests to your official booking page to confirm rates and availability.

☎

Manual Confirmation

If your team manages availability manually, the bot can capture the enquiry and hand it to staff to verify dates, prices, and reservation details.

Airbnb-specific capabilities, platform permissions, messaging restrictions, and booking-system integrations must be checked before implementation. The system will not promise unsupported direct Airbnb booking actions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Important details before we begin.

Will this work with Meta Business Suite?

The Facebook Page and messaging setup will be reviewed first. Depending on the required features and Meta's current capabilities, we can use Meta-supported tools or an automation platform such as ManyChat.

Can the automation check live availability?

Yes, if your booking system offers a supported integration or API. Otherwise, guests will be directed to your booking page or the team will confirm dates manually.

Can guests speak to a real person?

Yes. A clear support option will allow guests to request assistance. The exact handover method depends on your team's existing inbox and workflow.

Can we update room prices and photographs?

Yes. The handover will cover how to maintain the relevant room information, photos, links, policies, and FAQ content within the selected platform.

Will payments happen inside Messenger?

The recommended approach is to direct guests to your approved booking and payment provider. Messenger should explain the process rather than collect sensitive card details.

What do you need from us?

Access to the Facebook Page with the required permissions, room details, approved rates, photographs, house rules, booking links, payment instructions, and your current availability process.
