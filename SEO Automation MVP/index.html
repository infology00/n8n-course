Important technical point

OpenAI documents an OAI-SearchBot used to surface websites in ChatGPT search. That means crawlability and access matter; it is not accurate to promise that content can simply be “uploaded into ChatGPT” and instantly displayed. AI systems decide what they retrieve and show based on their own systems and available evidence.

Frequently asked

The questions a decision-maker will ask.

Will this guarantee Bright Force appears in ChatGPT?

No. AI systems control what they retrieve and display. The objective is to make Bright Force more understandable, crawlable and evidence-supported for relevant discovery opportunities, then monitor the actual results.

Can we publish AEO/GEO content to the website?

Yes. Approved answer blocks, FAQs, service content, location content and supporting assets can be published through the CMS. The important part is quality, accuracy and a controlled approval process.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. SEO remains the foundation. GEO extends the visibility strategy toward generative and AI-mediated discovery.

How do we know if it is working?

We compare baseline and post-launch data across Google Search Console, GA4, Business Profile performance and tracked AI discovery prompts.

How quickly can results appear?

Technical and indexing signals can change earlier. Competitive rankings and authority usually require sustained work. We use 30–90 day early indicators and 3–6+ month compounding expectations rather than promising a fixed ranking date.

What is the biggest advantage for Bright Force?

The same real business expertise — 24/7 emergency work, Level 2 ASP services, EV chargers, switchboards, strata and commercial work — can be turned into multiple discovery assets instead of being used only once on a single page.
