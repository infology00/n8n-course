Requirement Coverage

Every requirement mapped to a solution.

The following reference maps the requested business requirements to the proposed module and the corresponding demonstration screen.

Problem → Solution → Demonstration

REQ-01 · SALES
Retail & Wholesale Sales
Problem Sales for two businesses can become difficult to track when records are maintained separately.
Solution Business-aware POS and sales management with customer/dealer, payment and inventory linkage.
Demonstration Electronics POS + Sales screen + Live Transactions.
REQ-02 · SERVICE
Customer Complaints & Service
Problem Complaints can be lost without a central service history.
Solution Complaint → Job Card → Technician → Visit → Parts → Charges → Payment workflow.
Demonstration Repairs & Job Cards module.
REQ-03 · TECHNICIANS
Technician Management
Problem Management needs visibility into technician workload and profitability.
Solution Technician profiles, assigned jobs, visits, income, costs and profitability.
Demonstration Technician dashboard and profitability report.
REQ-04 · INVENTORY
Stock & Inventory
Problem Stock movement across purchases, sales and repairs needs one source of truth.
Solution Product catalogue, stock quantities, low-stock alerts and movement history.
Demonstration Inventory cards + stock movement.
REQ-05 · FINANCE
Payments, Cash & Outstanding
Problem Paid, partial and outstanding transactions need clear tracking.
Solution Payment ledger linked with invoices, customers, dealers and business accounts.
Demonstration Payments, outstanding and cash dashboard.
REQ-06 · REPORTING
Monthly Sales, Expenses & Profit
Problem Management needs a clear monthly picture instead of manually combining spreadsheets.
Solution Automated management reports with business-wise filtering.
Demonstration Reports & P&L module.
