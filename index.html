What the training covers

Eight practical areas.
Built around real work.

Examples below describe the learning areas. A corporate guide PDF can be added from the content manager when it is ready.

01

CRM Handover & Adoption

Map ownership, pipeline stages and daily use. Example: a sales team learns when to update a deal and who owns the next action.

People + process
02

Lead Response Tracking

Measure speed-to-lead and missed enquiries. Example: flag leads not contacted within the team's agreed response window.

Lead management
03

Conversation Quality

Review calls and messages for clarity, discovery and follow-up. Example: identify unanswered buyer questions and recurring objections.

Conversation review
04

Follow-up Automation

Design useful reminders and sequences with clear stop rules. Example: stop promotional follow-ups as soon as a meeting is booked.

Workflow design
05

Pipeline Visibility

Track stage movement, stalled deals and next steps. Example: review deals with no activity for seven days.

Sales operations
06

Conversion Improvement

Find friction between enquiry and close. Example: compare lead sources and stages where qualified prospects drop off.

Closing performance
07

Automation Monitoring

Check failures, exceptions and data quality. Example: alert an owner when a lead sync fails instead of silently losing it.

Reliability
08

Continuous Improvement

Use weekly reviews to improve scripts, routing and workflows. Example: update a process after a repeated customer complaint.
