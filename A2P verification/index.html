01 · Understand the basics

What is A2P 10DLC?

A2P means Application-to-Person messaging. 10DLC refers to standard 10-digit local phone numbers used for business messaging in the United States.

✓

Business identity

Registration helps associate business text messaging with a verifiable brand and its use case.

↔

Customer consent

Customers should understand what texts they are agreeing to receive and how to opt out.

▤

Campaign review

The campaign describes the messaging purpose, opt-in process and example messages for review.

Who needs this? Businesses sending messages to US recipients through US local 10-digit numbers may need A2P 10DLC registration. Other number types and countries may follow different rules.

Standard business / EIN route

Usually suitable for eligible registered entities with an EIN and matching business records.

  • Exact legal name and EIN
  • IRS confirmation documentation
  • Business profile and campaign details

Sole proprietor route

May be available to eligible individuals who meet the provider and carrier criteria.

  • Individual identity and contact details
  • Eligible US/Canadian details, where required
  • Verification using an eligible mobile number

Eligibility varies. International businesses may have options under standard brand registration using eligible business/tax details, depending on the provider and destination. Do not select a sole proprietor route unless you actually qualify.

05

Prepare your Privacy Policy

Explain data collection, SMS consent and data protection honestly.

Adapt the following example to the actual business, website, data collected and applicable law. Do not claim practices your business does not follow.

The SMS/data-sharing wording below is a compliance-oriented example based on this guide. It is not legal advice or a universal guarantee of approval.
1. Information collected and how it is used
We may collect information that you provide through our website, including your name, contact details, service inquiry and appointment preferences. We use this information to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, manage appointments and communicate with you in accordance with your choices and applicable law.
2. SMS consent and mobile information
If you provide your mobile number and separately opt in to SMS messages, we may use it to send the types of messages described at the point of consent, such as appointment reminders, service updates or promotional messages where separately authorized. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out by replying STOP or request help by replying HELP. 
No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
This statement must reflect your actual data-sharing practices. Do not sell, share or reuse SMS opt-in data for unrelated marketing.
3. Age, children and security
By opting into our SMS services, you represent that you are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through services intended for adults. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
4. Contact and policy footer
Include the real legal business name, physical business address, business phone, support email and links to your Terms & Conditions. Explain how users can ask privacy questions or request assistance. Review this policy against applicable privacy laws before publishing.
