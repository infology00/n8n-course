02 · Technology explained

What each tool does — and why it matters.

This architecture separates phone connectivity, speech, intelligence and business automation. Each technology has a defined job, so the complete system can be maintained, tested and expanded without putting everything into one complicated application.

☎

Twilio

The phone connection

Connects the system to real phone numbers and the telephone network. It can receive inbound calls, initiate outbound calls and provide call events to the application.

Business benefit: Customers call a real number, and approved outbound calling can be automated.
◖

Cartesia

The voice experience

Provides AI speech generation so the assistant can speak its responses with a natural-sounding voice. It is part of the audio experience, not the phone network itself.

Business benefit: A more natural spoken interaction instead of robotic recorded menu messages.
✳

AI Model

The conversation intelligence

Interprets what a customer is asking, uses business instructions and knowledge, and decides what response or next action is appropriate.

Business benefit: Customers can explain requests in their own words instead of navigating rigid menus.
⌘

n8n

The automation engine

Connects the voice assistant to APIs and business applications. It can look up records, create tasks, send notifications and run workflows based on call outcomes.

Business benefit: Less copy-pasting and fewer manual steps after every conversation.
▦

CRM / Business System

The customer record

Stores customer details, enquiry history, appointment information, call notes and follow-up status in the business's chosen platform.

Business benefit: Staff can see the customer history and continue the conversation with context.
◫

Admin Dashboard

The management layer

Gives authorised staff a place to review calls, transcripts, outcomes, workflow status, assistant settings and issues that require attention.

Business benefit: Managers can measure activity, investigate problems and improve the service.

How the components work together

Customer phone→Twilio→AI voice + model→n8n→CRM / Calendar→Admin dashboard
03 · Behind every call

From a spoken request to a completed business workflow.

A call should not end with just a voice response. The system can identify what the customer needs, check the permitted information, perform the relevant action and record the outcome for the business team.

01

Connect

Twilio receives the call and routes the audio to the configured voice application.

02

Listen

Speech recognition converts the customer's spoken words into information the AI can process.

03

Understand

The AI model interprets the request and determines the appropriate response within its instructions.

04

Take action

n8n runs approved workflows, such as looking up a record or creating a follow-up task.

05

Record

The outcome and call details are saved for staff review, reporting and the next customer interaction.

06 · Built for real operations

Automation with the right controls.

A production deployment should include secure API access, customer consent and calling compliance, clear escalation rules, error handling and testing. The assistant should know when to help and when to hand a conversation to a human team member.

🔐

Secure access

Protect credentials and customer information. Use role-based access and keep sensitive operations behind a secure backend.

🤝

Human handoff

Escalate complaints, unusual requests and sensitive situations to a trained staff member with the relevant conversation context.

📊

Continuous improvement

Review failed calls, conversation outcomes and workflow errors to improve the assistant's instructions and business processes.
