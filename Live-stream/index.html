02 / Multi-platform broadcasting

How do we go live on multiple platforms at once?

OBS Studio can act as the broadcast production layer. The avatar or video output is brought into the streaming scene, then the configured broadcast workflow distributes the stream to supported destinations.

✓OBS Studio: scenes, overlays, audio, and video output.
✓Multi-destination streaming: configure platform outputs or a suitable multistream service.
✓n8n orchestration: manage scripts, campaign details, job status, and operational notifications.
✓Platform checks: verify live-stream eligibility, stream keys, account permissions, and destination-specific requirements.

For a local OBS setup, the streaming computer must remain powered on and connected during the broadcast. A cloud-based or hosted broadcasting arrangement may change that requirement.

08 / Project clarity

Frequently asked questions

Will the same AI avatar go live on every platform at once?

The intended setup uses OBS and a supported multistream distribution path. Each target platform must support the selected broadcast method, and account eligibility, stream-key access, and any platform restrictions must be checked before implementation.

Does the computer need to stay on?

For a local OBS-based broadcast, yes: the computer must remain powered on, connected, and running the stream. A hosted or cloud broadcast architecture can reduce local-computer dependence but may require additional services.

Can viewers request a PDF, image, or video in comments?

The workflow can classify supported requests and create content tasks. Automatically generating and delivering each asset depends on the connected generation service, required reference materials, review rules, and the available delivery channel.

Can we publish videos and images from the portal?

The portal can organize creation and publishing actions. Direct publishing requires an approved integration for each destination; some platforms or media types may require manual review or upload.

Does the proposal include all software subscriptions?

The proposed project fee covers the agreed implementation scope. HeyGen or equivalent avatar services, AI usage, hosting, and any paid multistream or platform plans are separate unless specifically included in the final agreement.

Is all platform monetization guaranteed?

No. The system can support content and campaign operations, but monetization access and earnings are controlled by the relevant platform, account eligibility, audience performance, and commercial agreements.
