NEARBY · PRODUCT & SYSTEM BLUEPRINT
Project Overview · 2026

Discover nearby.
Connect smarter.

AWSZ Nearby is an AI-powered local business platform that connects customers with nearby shops and service providers. Customers can discover businesses, place orders and book appointments, while vendors manage operations, influencers earn eligible referral commissions, and admins control the complete platform.

AI Business Search Customer App Vendor Portal Influencer Portal Admin Portal Payments & Payouts

How the complete system works

A customer searches for a nearby business and places an order or booking. The vendor receives the request and updates its status. The system records transactions, calculates applicable platform fees and referral commissions, and tracks eligible payouts. The admin oversees users, businesses, subscriptions, payments and platform activity.

01 / CUSTOMER PORTAL

Customer App

Customers discover businesses, book services, order products and manage their activity.

AI-Powered Search

Customers search using everyday language instead of navigating complicated categories.

Example: User says, “Find a barber near me.” AI returns relevant available business listings.

Nearby Businesses

Displays relevant shops based on the customer's selected or permitted location.

Example: A customer selects an area and sees nearby milk shops and salons.

Business Profiles

Shows business information, services, products, prices, opening hours and location.

Example: A customer opens a barber profile to check haircut prices and timings.

Bookings & Appointments

Customers choose an available service and time slot.

Example: Customer books a 5 PM haircut; the vendor receives the appointment request.

Product Orders

Customers order products from listed businesses.

Example: Customer orders two litres of milk; the milk shop receives the order details.

Cart & Checkout

Combines selected products, quantities, delivery charges and applicable discounts before confirmation.

Example: Customer adds milk and bread to the cart and reviews the total before ordering.

Online Payment

Allows customers to pay through an integrated and supported payment gateway.

Example: Customer pays Rs. 2,000; the gateway returns a payment status that the system records.

Cash on Delivery

Offers cash payment where the platform and vendor support it.

Example: Customer selects COD, and the vendor receives an order marked for cash collection.

Order & Booking History

Customers can review previous and upcoming orders or appointments.

Example: Customer opens an order to check whether it has been dispatched or delivered.

Notifications

Provides relevant booking, order and payment updates.

Example: Vendor accepts a booking; the customer receives a confirmation notification.

Reviews & Ratings

Allows eligible customers to rate their experience with a business.

Example: After a completed haircut, the customer leaves a five-star review.

Profile & Saved Addresses

Customers manage contact information and delivery addresses.

Example: Customer saves a home address and selects it at the next checkout.

Customer Wallet & Credits

If implemented, the wallet records eligible platform credits and refunds.

Example: An approved platform credit appears in the customer's wallet balance.

Referrals & Favorites

Customers can save businesses and use available referral offers.

Example: Customer saves a favourite milk shop or applies an eligible referral code.
02 / VENDOR PORTAL

Vendor Management

Business owners manage their listings, products, bookings, orders, subscriptions and earnings.

Vendor Registration & Verification

Collects business details and supports admin review before activation.

Example: A milk shop submits its details; the admin reviews and approves the application.

Business Profile Management

Vendors manage their business description, address, contact details and hours.

Example: A barber updates the shop's opening hours and phone number.

Products & Services

Vendors create and maintain their product catalogue and service menu.

Example: A milk shop adds one-litre milk; a barber adds a haircut service.

Pricing, Stock & Availability

Manages prices, product stock and available services or appointment slots.

Example: A vendor marks an unavailable product out of stock.

Booking Management

Vendors accept, reject or reschedule appointment requests.

Example: Barber accepts the 5 PM booking; the customer receives the updated status.

Order Management

Vendors manage orders from receipt through preparation and fulfilment.

Example: Milk shop accepts an order and marks it ready for delivery.

Customer & Order Details

Displays information required to fulfil a confirmed booking or order.

Example: An accepted delivery order displays the relevant customer contact and delivery address.

Vendor Subscription

Displays the current plan, trial status, renewal date and available upgrades.

Example: A vendor upgrades from Basic to Pro and sees the updated plan status.

Sales & Performance

Summarises orders, bookings, revenue and business performance.

Example: Vendor checks how many orders were completed this month.

Vendor Earnings

Shows sales, platform fees, refunds, adjustments and the resulting payable balance.

Example: Rs. 10,000 sales minus Rs. 1,000 platform fee gives an initial Rs. 9,000 payable before other adjustments.

Vendor Payouts

Tracks eligible balances, payout requests and settlement statuses.

Example: Vendor requests an eligible payout; after the transfer is verified, its status changes to Paid.

Promotions & Reviews

Vendors manage supported discounts and review responses.

Example: A barber publishes a weekend offer and responds to a customer's review.

Staff Access

Where enabled, vendors can give staff limited permissions.

Example: A receptionist manages bookings without accessing financial settings.
03 / INFLUENCER PORTAL

Influencer & Referral System

Influencers promote participating businesses and track eligible referrals and commissions.

Registration & Approval

Influencers register and receive access after any required approval.

Example: An influencer submits an application and receives approval from the admin.

Referral Links & Codes

Provides unique links or codes for tracking eligible referrals.

Example: Influencer shares a unique AWSZ link in an Instagram post.

Campaign Management

Shows eligible vendors, campaigns, promotional assets and campaign rules.

Example: Influencer selects a participating milk shop's promotion to share.

Referral Tracking

Tracks supported link visits, registrations and attributed conversions.

Example: A customer visits through the influencer's link; the system records the referral.

Commission Calculation

Calculates commission using the configured campaign and qualifying-order rules.

Example: A qualifying Rs. 2,000 order at 5% commission generates a Rs. 100 commission entry.

Influencer Earnings

Separates pending, approved, payable and paid commissions.

Example: Influencer checks which commissions are approved and available for payout.

Influencer Payouts

Supports payout requests and tracks payment processing through the configured provider.

Example: Influencer requests an eligible Rs. 3,000 payout and tracks its status.

Campaign Analytics

Reports on clicks, conversions and eligible orders.

Example: Influencer compares two campaigns to see which generated more qualifying orders.
04 / ADMIN PORTAL

Platform Administration

The admin portal controls approvals, users, businesses, subscriptions, transactions and platform settings.

Admin Dashboard

Summarises users, vendors, influencers, orders and financial activity.

Example: Admin sees total active vendors and today's bookings.

User Management

Manages customer accounts, account status and support-related actions.

Example: Admin reviews an account following a reported issue.

Vendor & Influencer Approvals

Reviews applications and controls access according to platform rules.

Example: Admin approves a business and separately approves an influencer.

Business Categories & Listings

Manages categories, listing visibility and business information.

Example: Admin creates a Milk Shops category and reviews submitted listings.

Subscription Plans

Configures vendor plans, pricing, trials, renewals and plan limits.

Example: Admin sets a monthly price for the Pro vendor plan.

Orders & Bookings Monitoring

Allows authorised admins to inspect order and appointment records.

Example: Admin checks the booking history to investigate a complaint.

Platform Fees & Commissions

Defines applicable transaction fees and influencer commission rules.

Example: Admin configures a 5% commission for an eligible campaign.

Payments, Refunds & Settlements

Monitors transaction records, authorised refunds and vendor or influencer payouts.

Example: Admin reviews a pending vendor settlement and checks its gateway status.

Promotions & Coupons

Manages promotional rules, discount codes and campaign eligibility.

Example: Admin configures a campaign with a defined expiry date.

Reports & Analytics

Provides reports on orders, revenue, fees, refunds, subscriptions and commissions.

Example: Admin reviews monthly platform revenue and pending payouts.

Roles, Permissions & Audit Logs

Controls staff access and records important administrative actions.

Example: Finance staff can review transactions but cannot change AI settings.

Complaints & Fraud Monitoring

Flags suspicious activity and supports investigation of reported issues.

Example: Multiple suspicious referrals are flagged for admin review.
05 / FINANCE

Payments, Earnings & Payouts

A shared financial system records customer payments, vendor balances, platform revenue and influencer commissions.

Payment Gateway

Processes supported online payments and returns transaction statuses.

Example: Customer pays for an order; the backend verifies the gateway response.

Transaction Ledger

Maintains linked records of payments, fees, refunds, commissions and settlements.

Example: An order payment and its later refund remain traceable in the ledger.

Vendor Payables

Calculates the amount owed to a vendor after applicable fees and adjustments.

Example: Rs. 2,000 sale minus Rs. 200 platform fee gives an initial Rs. 1,800 payable.

Influencer Commissions

Tracks qualifying referrals and applies configured commission rules.

Example: An eligible Rs. 2,000 sale at 5% generates a Rs. 100 commission entry.

Refunds & Adjustments

Records approved refunds, cancellations and relevant balance changes.

Example: A Rs. 500 refund is linked to the original order and payment.

Vendor Settlements

Tracks eligible vendor balances through payout processing and reconciliation.

Example: An approved payout is sent through the configured provider and reconciled against its result.

Influencer Payouts

Tracks approved commissions and their payout status.

Example: After a qualifying order clears the required checks, its commission becomes eligible for payout.

Financial Reconciliation

Matches internal transactions with gateway and payout records.

Example: Admin identifies a successful customer payment whose settlement is still pending.
Payment rule: Actual money transfers require a compatible payment and payout provider, verified accounts and backend integration. A dashboard status alone does not transfer funds.
06 / SUBSCRIPTIONS

Vendor Plans & Billing

Controls how businesses access paid platform features and how their subscriptions are billed.

Subscription Plans

Offers configured plans with different features, limits and prices.

Example: Basic includes a limited listing; Pro includes additional promotional features.

Free Trials

Provides eligible vendors access for a defined trial period.

Example: A vendor starts a 14-day trial and receives an expiry reminder.

Recurring Billing

Processes renewals through a supported gateway when recurring billing is enabled.

Example: A successful monthly renewal updates the subscription expiry date.

Upgrade, Downgrade & Expiry

Handles plan changes, renewal status and configured restrictions after expiry.

Example: An expired trial prompts the vendor to select a paid plan.

Invoices & Billing History

Records subscription charges, renewal attempts and invoice details.

Example: Vendor views the invoice for the previous billing period.
07 / AI ASSISTANT

AI Search & Recommendations

The AI interprets requests and works with platform data to help users find relevant businesses.

Intent Detection

Identifies whether the customer wants a business, product, service or appointment.

Example: “I need a haircut today” is interpreted as a service and time-related request.

Location & Category Matching

Matches the request against available business listings and location information.

Example: A request for nearby milk shops returns relevant listed businesses.

Filters & Recommendations

Uses available price, rating, distance and availability data to narrow results.

Example: User requests a haircut under Rs. 1,000 and sees matching listings with known prices.

Booking & Order Assistance

Guides customers through selection and checks availability through the backend.

Example: AI checks a requested time slot before the customer confirms a booking.

AI Provider & Usage Settings

Allows authorised administrators to configure the AI provider and monitor usage.

Example: Admin reviews AI request volume and estimated API costs.
Data accuracy: The AI must use available platform data or authorised sources. Live prices, stock and booking availability must be verified before they are presented as confirmed.

Order Fulfilment

Manages the delivery process after a vendor accepts a product order.

Delivery Method

Offers the delivery methods configured for a business.

Example: Customer selects home delivery or store pickup where available.

Delivery Charges

Calculates charges according to configured rules.

Example: Checkout adds the applicable Rs. 200 delivery fee.

Delivery Status

Records preparation, dispatch and delivery progress.

Example: Vendor marks an order dispatched and the customer receives an update.

Delivery History

Stores completed, cancelled and relevant delivery records.

Example: Customer checks the final status of a previous order.

Reviews, Complaints & Disputes

Provides a structured process for feedback, support and issue resolution.

Ratings & Reviews

Collects feedback from eligible customers after a transaction.

Example: Customer leaves a rating after a completed haircut appointment.

Support Tickets

Creates trackable requests for customer, vendor or influencer support.

Example: Customer reports a missing order and receives a ticket reference.

Dispute Management

Allows authorised staff to investigate order, booking or payment disputes.

Example: Admin reviews the order record and vendor response to resolve a complaint.

Resolution Tracking

Tracks cases through open, under-review and resolved states.

Example: A resolved ticket is updated and the customer receives a status notification.

Booking & Order Alerts

Payment & Payout Alerts

Subscription Reminders

Notifies vendors about trial expiry and renewal events.

Email, SMS & In-App Messages

Uses the configured communication providers and user preferences.

Influencer Portal Referrals · Commissions · Payouts

Shared Database

Example: A vendor accepts a booking; the shared booking record updates so the customer and admin see the correct status.

Maps & Location Services

Supports location-based discovery and map display through a configured provider.

Example: Customer filters business listings by distance from a selected location.

AI & Automation APIs

Connects AI models, business search, notifications and approved workflows.

Example: A confirmed booking triggers a configured notification workflow.

Security, Permissions & Backups

Protects account access and sensitive records, while supporting monitoring and recovery.

Example: An influencer can see their own commissions but cannot access another influencer's financial records.
