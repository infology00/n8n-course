Project overview · Phase 02

Automations that handle the follow-up

Once the CRM foundation is confirmed, these workflows can run from form submission, appointment status, payment status, contact activity or pipeline stage changes.

01

Form Submission & Source Tagging

Create/update contact, tag the form/page source, store jurisdiction, matter and lawyer, create/update opportunity, route the lead.

Trigger: GHL form submitted
02

Consultation Booked

Immediate confirmation; reminders 24 hours, 2 hours and 30 minutes before; preparation email with documents to prepare and topics for the lawyer.

Trigger: Appointment booked
03

No-Show Recovery

SMS within 15 minutes with rebooking link, then Day 1 and Day 3 follow-ups. Stop if the lead rebooks or opts out.

Trigger: Appointment marked no-show
04

Missed Call Text-Back

Send a text within about one minute inviting the caller to reply or book a consultation.

Trigger: Missed call
05

Hired Client & Agreement

When a lead becomes Hired, send the relevant retainer/agreement using the firm's chosen path: with payment link or without it.

Trigger: Pipeline stage = Hired
06

Abandoned Booking Recovery

If booking payment is not completed: SMS after 30 minutes, email after 4 hours and final SMS the next day. Stop once completed.

Trigger: Booking started, payment incomplete
07

Old Lead Reactivation

Approved SMS to eligible older leads first; email non-responders. Respect consent, opt-outs and the firm's approved legal messaging.

Trigger: Approved one-off campaign
08

Channel Escalation

After three ignored emails, switch to SMS; if still cold, use a supported permitted ringless voicemail provider, then a final plain-text email.

Trigger: No response threshold reached
09

Delayed Application / Mandamus Nurture

Share firm-approved information about processing delays and mandamus options with relevant leads; no promises about outcomes.

Trigger: Eligible delayed-application lead
10

Document Collection Drip

Reminders on Day 3, Day 7 and Day 14 for outstanding documents; staff task on Day 21.

Trigger: Hired client with missing documents
11

Lead SLA Alert

During configured business hours, alert the team at 45 minutes without human touch and alert a manager at one hour.

Trigger: New lead not actioned
12

Stale Pipeline Stage Sweeper

Nightly review of opportunities stuck longer than the agreed number of days; create a staff task and route to the correct nurture flow.

Trigger: Nightly scheduled check

Lawyer calendars & rules

Scope clarity: The simulator is interactive and demonstrates sample logic locally in the browser. It does not send real SMS/emails, create real GHL contacts, charge payments, or connect to Clio.
